# Pesly UX-laws audit — 2026-10-31

Audit of the web app against the Laws of UX (Fitts, Hick, Miller, Jakob, Tesler, Postel,
Von Restorff, Prägnanz/proximity, aesthetic-usability, Doherty, Zeigarnik, goal-gradient,
peak-end) plus the WCAG basics they depend on.

**Method.** The app ran locally (API + web dev server + PostgreSQL, fake rate and price
providers). A Playwright script registered a user, seeded 3 accounts and 15 movements, and visited
28 authenticated routes at 390×844 (phone) and 1280×800 (desktop). On each page it measured
horizontal overflow, controls under 44 px, controls without an accessible name, inputs without a
label, the heading outline, the font sizes in use, and input `type`/`inputmode`/`autocomplete`.
The screenshots were reviewed by hand, alongside the code.

**What passed everywhere (56 page × viewport checks):** no horizontal overflow; no control under
44 × 44 px (the only hit is the visually hidden skip link); every control has an accessible name;
every input has a label; exactly one `h1` per page; reduced-motion respected; the colour tokens
are contrast-tested. The baseline is strong. The findings below are mostly about information
architecture, cognitive load and consistency, not accessibility basics.

Severity: **P1** blocks or misleads at the moment of paying · **P2** clear friction · **P3** polish.

---

## P1

### 1. A missing stored rate blocks recording a peso expense — Tesler's law, Postel's law
- `features/movements/components/rate-field.tsx:33`: with no stored rate the rate field becomes
  `required`, even for an ARS expense on an ARS account. On a first install, or before the rate
  job has run, the user must look up and type an ARS-per-USD rate to save "$1.500 at the kiosk".
- The complexity of conversion belongs to the system: save the movement without a frozen rate
  and fill it in later from the stored history (and mark the movement as "rate pending"), or
  make the field optional for same-currency movements and show the hint only.

### 2. Amounts split mid-currency ("1.800.000,00 AR / S") — aesthetic-usability, Prägnanz
- Seen on the home KPI cards (phone and desktop) and the income-vs-expenses report summary.
  Caused by `break-words` on figures built with `formatMoney` outside the `Amount` component
  (`features/dashboard/components/kpi-cards.tsx:58`, `net-worth-card.tsx:139`, reports summary).
- Fix: render these figures with `Amount` (it already uses `whitespace-nowrap`), keep the
  number and currency on one line, and let the figure shrink (`text-heading` →
  `text-body` under a container query) instead of breaking.

### 3. Two spellings of the dollar: "USD" and "US$" — Jakob's law, consistency
- Home and movements show `1.200,00 USD` (`lib/format-amount.ts:19`); Accounts shows
  `1200,00 US$` (the `Amount` component calls `formatMoney` from `@pesly/shared`). Two
  formatters for the same money in one app.
- Fix: one formatter (the shared one) used everywhere; pick one label (USD is what the forms and
  copy use) and delete `lib/format-amount.ts#formatMoney`.

## P2

### 4. "More" is a flat list of 12 destinations — Hick's law, Miller's law
- Phone `/more`: Cuentas, Tarjetas, Presupuestos, Metas, Recurrentes, Inversiones, Categorías,
  Reportes, Avisos, Seguridad, Perfil, Notificaciones, then theme and sign-out, in one undivided
  list. The desktop sidebar already groups them ("Planificar", "Ajustes").
- Fix: reuse the same groups on phone (Money: accounts, cards, investments · Plan: budgets, goals,
  recurring, reports · Settings: profile, security, notifications, categories, theme). Accounts
  and cards are core daily objects; consider promoting one of them out of "More".

### 5. Four stacked filter layers on Movements — Hick's law
- Type chips (Todos/Gasto/Ingreso/Transferencia…, the last one cut off at the edge), then a
  "Filtros" disclosure, then period pills (Todo/Este mes/Mes pasado), then a Gastos/Ingresos
  toggle inside the summary. Two of them filter by type.
- Fix: one type control, one period control, everything else in the "Filtros" sheet; show active
  filters as removable chips. Remove the duplicate Gastos/Ingresos toggle or tie it to the type
  filter.

### 6. The same warning three times on Home — signal-to-noise, Miller's law
- With no rate: "Conversión no disponible…" under Patrimonio neto, again under Disponible, and a
  third card "Ningún punto se pudo convertir…". Plus two footnotes under the KPIs ("Comparado con
  el mismo período…", "Las compras con tarjeta cuentan…").
- Fix: one inline notice for the whole hero; move the methodology footnotes behind an info button
  or into the report.

### 7. Home is ~2,800 px tall on a phone with nine blocks — Miller's law, serial position
- Hero, conversion notice, three KPIs, quick actions, category donut with six rows, budgets,
  next 7 days, groups, insight. Quick actions (Gasto/Ingreso/Transferir/Cambio) sit below the
  first screen and duplicate the centred "+" in the bottom bar.
- Fix: order by daily value (balance → quick actions → this month → upcoming); collapse the
  donut to the top 3 categories + "ver todas"; hide empty cards ("Crear un presupuesto",
  "No vence nada", groups at 0,00) behind a single "first steps" card instead of four empty
  blocks.

### 8. New budget: a 28-checkbox tree, fully expanded — Miller's law (chunking), Hick's law
- `/budgets/new` is ~2,370 px on a phone; every parent and subcategory is open.
- Fix: collapsed parent rows with a count ("Comida · 0 de 3"), checking the parent selects its
  children, a search field, and a segmented control instead of the native `<select>` for the
  two-option currency.

### 9. Account cards: four adjacent icon actions with delete next to archive — Fitts's law (errors)
- Each account card has edit, link, archive and delete side by side in the thumb zone. Delete
  asks for confirmation (good), but the pattern repeats on every card and makes the list heavy.
- Fix: one overflow menu (⋯) per card, delete last and separated, styled destructive. The
  account type label is truncated ("Cuenta ba…") next to the currency badge — let it wrap or drop
  the badge, the amount already carries the currency.

### 10. The PWA is not installable as an app — Jakob's law (platform conventions)
- `app/manifest.ts` has no `icons`, `theme_color` or `background_color`, and there is no
  `viewport` export (`theme-color`, `viewport-fit=cover`). The install prompt, the splash screen
  and the status-bar tint all depend on them. (Already raised on 2026-10-13, still open.)

## P3

11. **Truncated labels.** Theme segmented control "Siste…" in More; category tile "Impuestos y…"
    in the movement form; type tab "Cambio de moneda" wraps to two lines. Shorter labels
    ("Auto", "Impuestos", "Cambio") or a wider control.
12. **Chart axis text at 10 px** (`features/reports/income-expenses/month-bars.tsx:114,153`),
    below the 11 px floor the app uses elsewhere (`--text-nav`). Use the caption token (12 px).
13. **Tone of voice.** The Spanish catalog uses *tú* (≈130 strings) but a few strings use *vos*
    ("Podés…", "Ingresá…", `messages/es.json:1516,1524,1570,2142`). Pick one; for an
    Argentine product *vos* is the local convention (Jakob), but it must be consistent.
14. **Storage warning on first visit.** "Tu navegador puede borrar los datos…" shows on every
    screen from the first session, before the user has stored anything worth losing. Show it only
    when there is unsynced data or after a persistence request is denied.
15. **Movement list headers repeat the only row's amount** when a day has one movement. Show the
    day total only when the day has two or more movements.
16. **Frequent categories first** in the movement form (Hick's law): the grid always shows all
    categories in a fixed order; put the 4–8 most used first.

## Mobile pass (touch emulation)

**Method.** A second Playwright run with Playwright's `iPhone 14` profile (390×664 CSS px,
DPR 3, `isMobile`, `hasTouch`), then iPhone SE width (320×568), landscape (844×390) and dark
mode. 23 authenticated routes, including detail and form screens the first pass did not reach
(movement edit, group detail, group expense, goal detail, card detail, card expense,
installments). Per page: input font size (iOS zooms on focus below 16 px), `type`/`inputmode`/
`autocomplete`/`enterkeyhint`, presence of `:active` feedback, native `<select>` option counts,
position of the primary action, and content left under the bottom bar at the end of the page.

**Passed:** no horizontal overflow even at 320 px; nothing left hidden under the bottom bar at the
end of any page; auth fields have the right `type` and `autocomplete` (`email`,
`current-password`, `new-password`, `name`); money fields use `inputmode="decimal"`; the
personal movement form has its own keypad (`inputmode="none"`), so the system keyboard does not
cover half of the screen.

### M1 (P1). Zero touch feedback — Doherty threshold, feedback
- `grep active:` over `apps/web/src`: 0 classes. Tailwind's preflight also sets
  `-webkit-tap-highlight-color: transparent` (measured on `body`). Every button, row and tab
  changes only on `hover:`, which never fires on touch. A tap gives no visual response until
  the next screen renders; on a slow network it reads as "didn't work" and invites double taps
  (double expense records).
- Fix: add `active:` states to the shared primitives only (`buttonVariants`, `Chip`,
  `PillTabs`, `listRowVariants`, `circularActionVariants`, the bottom-bar links): e.g.
  `active:scale-[0.97] active:bg-accent/80`, under `motion-safe:` for the scale. Ten files, no
  feature changes.

### M2 (P1). The bottom bar sits on the iPhone home indicator — Fitts, platform conventions
- `pb-safe` uses `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, but there is no `viewport` export with
  `viewportFit: 'cover'`; measured meta is `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`. Without
  `viewport-fit=cover`, iOS keeps the inset at 0 inside an installed PWA, so the bar's labels
  collide with the home-indicator gesture area. No `theme-color` either (status bar is not tinted).
- Fix: `export const viewport = { viewportFit: 'cover', themeColor: [{ media:
  '(prefers-color-scheme: light)', color: … }, { media: '(prefers-color-scheme: dark)', … }] }`
  in `app/[locale]/layout.tsx` (colors from the tokens).

### M3 (P1). The save button is two screens away — Fitts, goal-gradient
- Primary action position on iPhone 14 (viewport 664 px): new movement `Guardar movimiento` at
  y=1425 of 1795; edit at 1533; group expense at 1189; budget at 2071; recurring at 1075. The
  most frequent task in the app (record an expense standing at the till) needs scrolling past the
  keypad and the category grid to save.
- Fix: a sticky action bar for forms on phones (`sticky bottom-0` above the bottom nav, with
  `pb-safe`), or hide the bottom nav on full-screen forms (they already have a back link) and
  put the save button where the nav was. Also: the first screen of the new-movement form is
  title + description + type tabs + amount; drop the description on phones.

### M4 (P2). Search field at 14 px makes iOS zoom in — platform conventions
- Movements search (`movements` page) renders at 14 px, so Safari zooms the page on focus and
  leaves it zoomed. In landscape (≥ 768 px wide, the `md:` breakpoint), **every** input drops to
  14 px through `md:text-small` in `components/ui/input.tsx`, so on a phone in landscape every
  field zooms.
- Fix: keep 16 px for inputs on touch devices: replace `md:text-small` with
  `[@media(pointer:fine)]:text-small` (or drop it), and give the search field the same rule.

### M5 (P2). Native pickers with long lists — Hick's law
- Native `<select>` with 29 categories (card expense, installments, recurring, group expense),
  34 in the movements filter, and **419 time zones** in Profile. On iOS this is a scroll wheel;
  finding "Supermercado" or "America/Argentina/Buenos_Aires" takes many flicks.
- The personal movement form already has a good icon grid for categories: reuse it (or a
  searchable sheet) in every category picker, so the same task looks the same everywhere
  (Jakob, internal consistency). Pre-select the time zone from the device and show the list only
  on "change", with search.

### M6 (P2). The group expense form is a different form for the same job — Jakob, consistency
- Personal expense: own keypad, account card, category grid. Group expense: system keyboard,
  five native selects (currency, payer, account, personal category, group category), a
  non-full-width save button. Labels are doubled: "Monto" (section) + "Monto" (field), "Fecha y
  quién pagó" + "Fecha" (screenshot `group-expense-new`).
- Fix: build it from the same pieces as the movement form; drop the duplicated section legend
  when the section has one field.

### M7 (P2). Landscape leaves ~40 % of the screen for content
- At 844×390 the top bar (64 px) and the bottom bar with its raised button (~160 px including
  the FAB overhang) take more than half of the height; the new-movement form shows only its title
  and type tabs.
- Fix: in landscape on phones (`@media (orientation: landscape) and (max-height: 500px)`),
  make the top bar non-sticky and drop the bar labels / FAB lift, or use the side menu.

### M8 (P3). Smaller mobile items
- Numeric day fields use `type="number"` (`cards/new`, card detail, recurring `dayOfMonth`,
  `reminderDays`): spinners, scroll-to-change and accepts `e`. The installments field already
  uses `type="text" inputmode="numeric"`; use that everywhere.
- No `enterkeyhint` anywhere: set `next` on intermediate fields and `done`/`send` on the last
  one, so the keyboard's return key moves through the form.
- The home first screen spends ~170 px on the sync pill + the brand row ("Pesly" logo) before
  the balance; the brand is already in the app icon. Move the logo into the top bar or drop it
  on phones, so the KPIs and quick actions reach the first screen.
- Icon-only actions rely on `title` for their tooltip (`IconAction`, sign-out icon), which touch
  devices never show. The accessible name is fine; for sighted touch users, prefer a labelled
  overflow menu (see #9).
- Date fields are native `datetime-local`. Fine on mobile, but the movement form would benefit
  from "Hoy / Ayer" chips before the picker (most entries are today).

## Laws already well served (keep)

- **Fitts:** 44 px minimum enforced on every control; centred raised "+" in the bottom bar; full
  width primary buttons; a numeric keypad on the amount field.
- **Jakob:** bottom tab bar on phone, side menu on desktop, back links with the parent's name.
- **Von Restorff:** one saturated surface per screen (the navy hero); primary actions are the only
  filled buttons.
- **Doherty / perceived speed:** skeletons on every list, offline queue with "Al día" status, LCP
  budgets tested in e2e.
- **Zeigarnik / goal-gradient:** first-steps dashboard and goal progress bars.
- **Accessibility basics:** skip link, labelled inputs, `aria-pressed` on toggles, sign plus
  colour for income/expense, reduced motion, contrast tests in both themes.

## Suggested order

1. #1 rate not blocking, #2 amount wrapping, #3 one currency formatter (small, high trust).
2. Mobile quick wins: M1 `active:` states in the primitives, M2 + #10 viewport/manifest,
   M4 16 px inputs, M8 `type="number"` and `enterkeyhint`; #11–#13 copy and label fixes.
3. M3 sticky save on forms (biggest mobile gain for the core task), M6 group-expense form.
4. #4 grouped More, #5 filters, #6–#7 home simplification, #8 budget categories, #9 account
   actions, M5 pickers, M7 landscape (design work, one PR each).

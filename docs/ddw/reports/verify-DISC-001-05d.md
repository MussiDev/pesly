  5. Concurrent edits are last-write-wins and both are logged with their before values (spec D10, open question 5); there is no version check, and the conflicts of offline edits belong to DISC-001-04e.

DISABLE TRIGGER` or `DROP TRIGGER` by the table owner, or `session_replication_role` by a superuser (SAST I-2).

## Functional requirements
- ✅ FR-01 — create with name, target, currency, optional date: `CreateGoal` and `DrizzleGoalRepository.create`; proven by AC-01, AC-02 and AC-17.
- ✅ FR-02 — link to an own account of the same currency at creation: `AccountBalances.findLinkable` and the composite key `goals_account_owner_fk`; proven by AC-03 and AC-18.
- ✅ FR-03 — progress of a linked goal is the account balance: `DrizzleAccountBalances.balancesOf`; proven by AC-04 and AC-21.
- ✅ FR-04 — contributions on an unlinked goal: `RecordGoalEntry` / `recordEntry`; proven by AC-05, AC-19 and AC-20.
- ✅ FR-05 — withdrawals on an unlinked goal, never beyond progress: proven by AC-06 and AC-07.
- ✅ FR-06 — progress of an unlinked goal is contributions minus withdrawals: `progressOf` (one grouped statement); proven by AC-08.
- ✅ FR-07 — progress and percentage in every response: `percentageOf` and `goalResponseSchema`; proven by AC-09 and AC-21.
- ✅ FR-08 — monthly amount needed, current month in the user's zone, rounded up: `monthsLeft`, `monthlyAmountNeeded`, `DrizzleUserTimeZone`; proven by AC-10, AC-11, AC-25 and AC-26.
- ✅ FR-09 — reached state: `goalStatus`; proven by AC-12.
- ✅ FR-10 — edit name, target and date: `UpdateGoal`; proven by AC-13 and AC-22.
- ✅ FR-11 — delete a goal with its entries: `DeleteGoal`, cascade key; proven by AC-14, plus `erasure-step.test.ts` "deletes only the user's goals and entries (FR-11)".
- ✅ FR-12 — owner-only read, edit, delete: scope on every repository method; proven by AC-15 and AC-16.
- ✅ FR-13 — a linked account cannot be deleted: restricting key plus `AccountLinkedToGoal`; proven by AC-23.
- ✅ FR-14 — at most 100 goals per user: advisory lock plus count in one transaction; proven by AC-24.
